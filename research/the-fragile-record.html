The Fragile Record

The S&P 500 printed a record this week.

Every pillar in our twelve-pillar framework is flashing late cycle.

That gap is the story. Price is the last variable to turn. The flows under it turned quarters ago.

This edition runs the diagnostic layer pillar by pillar. Sixty charts across twelve pillars, tracking where the fragility is concentrated, where it is not, and how the internals are diagnosing the distribution in real time. Composite synthesis returns next Chartbook. For this one, we let the indicators do the work.

What the data says

Labor is freezing, not cracking. Quits at 2.0%. Hires at 3.2%. Layoffs still historically low at 1.1%. The market isn’t breaking, it’s seizing up. Temp help is 38 months inside the recession signal. At some point the firing means something.
