About

Bob Sheehan, CFA, CMT

Founder & Chief Investment Officer

"The Lighthouse" was the name of Bob's house in college, where he first began studying finance and economics. Over the years it evolved into a research firm built on quantitative rigor and systematic macro analysis, serving portfolio managers, advisory firms, and business leaders who need to understand what's actually happening beneath the headlines.

Bob's neurodivergent wiring is core to the firm's edge. Most analysts specialize in one domain. His ADHD-driven intellectual restlessness pushes him across the full macro landscape: Fed plumbing, labor economics, credit markets, Treasury microstructure, crypto integration, and quantitative modeling. The result is depth across domains, and the ability to see connections specialists miss by staying in silos.

Prior to founding Lighthouse Macro, Bob served as VP of Data & Analytics at EquiLend, Associate Portfolio Manager at Bank of America Private Bank ($4.5B multi-asset AUM; SGS equity sleeve ~$1.2B at peak, 2.35 Sortino, 103% upside capture, 76% downside capture vs S&P 500), and Senior Research Analyst at Strom Capital Management.
